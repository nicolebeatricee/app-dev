🌸 My Favorite Series: The Summer I Turned Pretty
📖 Overview

The Summer I Turned Pretty is a romantic drama series based on the book by Jenny Han.
It tells the story of Belly Conklin, who spends every summer at Cousins Beach with her family and friends — but one summer, everything starts to change.

💞 Main Characters

Belly Conklin – The main character who experiences love and heartbreak.

Conrad Fisher – The quiet and mysterious older brother.

Jeremiah Fisher – The fun and outgoing younger brother.

Laurel Park – Belly’s caring and supportive mother.

Susannah Fisher – The mother of Conrad and Jeremiah, who loves Belly like her own.

🌅 Favorite Quote

“For me, it was always Conrad.” – Belly Conklin

💗 Why I Love It

I love this series because it shows the beauty of growing up, first love, and the importance of family.
The beach setting and summer vibes make it feel warm and nostalgic.

Season 2 – Dealing with loss and complicated love
